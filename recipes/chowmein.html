Nepali Chowmein Recipe

Description

Nepali Chowmein (चाउमिन) is one of the most popular street foods in Nepal. Stir-fried noodles loaded with crunchy vegetables, aromatic ginger-garlic, soy sauce, and local spices deliver a delicious savory flavor with a slight kick of heat.

Ingredients

  • 200g Chowmein Noodles (boiled and drained)
  • 2 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • 1 cup Veggies (shredded cabbage, carrots, bell peppers, sliced onions)
  • 2 tbsp Dark Soy Sauce & 1 tbsp Tomato Ketchup
  • 1/2 tsp Chili powder & 1/2 tsp Cumin powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil noodles according to package instructions, drain, rinse with cold water, and toss with 1 tsp oil to prevent sticking.
  2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
  3. Add sliced onions and ginger-garlic paste; stir-fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add shredded cabbage, carrots, and bell peppers; stir-fry on high heat for 2–3 minutes so they stay crunchy.
  5. Stir in soy sauce, ketchup, chili powder, cumin powder, salt, and pepper.
  6. Add the boiled noodles and toss continuously on high heat for 2–3 minutes until evenly coated with sauce and spices.
  7. Garnish with chopped spring onions and serve piping hot with hot sauce!
