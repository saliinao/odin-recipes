Chicken Masu Recipe

Description

In Nepali cuisine, Masu (मासु) simply translates to "meat curry." It is a beloved staple made with chicken (Kukhura ko Masu), mutton/goat (Khasi ko Masu), or buff (Baff ko Masu).

Unlike heavy cream-based curries, traditional Nepali masu is cooked in mustard oil with aromatic spices, fresh garlic, ginger, tomatoes, and cilantro—resulting in a rich, deeply savory, and slightly spicy dish that pairs perfectly with Dal Bhat (rice and lentils) or Roti.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg (2.2 lbs) Meat (Chicken with bone)
  • 3-4 tbsp Mustard oil
  • 2 large Red onions
  • 2 medium Tomatoes

Steps

  1. Heat 4 tbsp oil in a pot. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 bay leaf, cinnamon stick, cardamom, and cloves for 20 seconds
  2. Add 2 sliced onions and cook until golden brown (6–8 mins).
  3. Add 1 kg chicken, half the turmeric, and a pinch of salt. Cook for 8–10 mins until browned.
  4. Add 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tbsp coriander powder, 1 tsp chili powder, and remaining turmeric. Cook for 2 mins.
  5. Stir in 2 chopped tomatoes, cover, and cook for 5 mins until soft and oil separates
  6. Add 1 cup warm water (for gravy/jhol), cover, and simmer for 10–12 mins until cooked.
  7. Stir in 1 tsp garam masala and top with fresh cilantro. Serve hot with rice!
