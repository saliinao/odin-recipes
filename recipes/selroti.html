Sel Roti Recipe

Description

Sel Roti (सेल रोटी) is a traditional Nepali ring-shaped sweet rice bread prepared during major festivals like Dashain and Tihar. Crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, it is made from fermented rice batter flavored with ghee, sugar, and cardamom, then deep-fried to perfection.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Tayar Chawal (Soaked raw rice, coarsely ground)
  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 2 tbsp Ghee or Butter
  • 1 tsp Cardamom & Clove powder
  • 1/2 cup Milk (or warm water, as needed)
  • Cooking oil or ghee (for deep frying)

Steps

  1. Soak rice overnight, drain excess water, and blend coarsely into a thick paste.
  2. Mix the rice flour with sugar, ghee, cardamom powder, and milk to make a smooth, pouring batter (not too runny). Let it rest for 1–2 hours.
  3. Heat oil/ghee in a wide, flat-bottomed deep pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Pour the batter carefully into hot oil in a ring/donut shape using your hands or a funnel/squeezable bottle.
  5. Fry the Sel Roti until the bottom turns golden brown, then flip using long wooden skewers (jhir).
  6. Cook both sides until golden and crispy, then drain excess oil and serve warm with tea or chicken curry!
